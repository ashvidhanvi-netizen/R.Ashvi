The Last Dragon's Promise

Deep within the Misty Mountains lived the last dragon, named Arion. For hundreds of years, he protected a magical crystal that kept peace across the kingdom.

One day, a young traveler discovered the dragon's hidden cave. Instead of seeking treasure, the traveler promised to protect the crystal and keep its secret safe from those who wanted to misuse its power.

Trusting the traveler, Arion passed on his responsibility and flew into the sky for the final time. The kingdom remained peaceful, proving that courage and honesty are the greatest treasures of all.
