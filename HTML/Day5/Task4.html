A Journey Through the Cursed Woods

A brave explorer named Leo entered the mysterious Werewolf Forest in search of an ancient secret.

The villagers believed the forest was haunted, but Leo remained fearless and continued his journey.

Deep inside the forest, he discovered a hidden cave beneath an old oak tree that no one had seen before.

Leo first carried a broken map, but he later found an ancient magical map that revealed the secret path.

At midnight, the werewolf appeared under the full moon. The cave was nearly 52 kilometers away, and Leo carried a bottle of H2O during his adventure.
