My First Step into the IT World

My name is Ashvi. I completed my bachelor's degree in Pharmaceutical Technology. During my college days, I realized that my core field was not suitable for me because I was physically unable to continue working in that field. That made me think about choosing a different career. In my second year of college, I became interested in the IT field. However, I could not join any coaching classes because I wanted to complete my degree first. After completing my studies, I started searching for a good institute to learn programming. I searched on the internet, spoke to many people, and asked my friends for suggestions. Many of them recommended Excel Institute because of its good teaching and positive reviews. After learning more about it, I decided to join. Coding is completely new to me. At first, I felt nervous because I had no programming knowledge. But now, I feel excited because every day I am learning something new. I know the journey may be difficult, but I am ready to work hard. I believe that with regular practice, patience, and dedication, I can become a good software developer. This is my first step into the IT world, and I am excited to see where this journey takes me.
