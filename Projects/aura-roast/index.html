The Origin of AURA ROAST

Our Story

At AURA ROAST, we believe that life's most meaningful moments often unfold over a warm cup or a chilled glass. What began as an artisan roastery driven by a passion for pure organic coffee has grown into a celebration of all everyday beverage moments.

Today, we curate honest, beautifully crafted drinks: single-estate coffees roasted gently to caramelize natural sugars, whole-leaf mountain teas infused with real herbs and citrus, and refreshing cold drinks crafted to revitalize your day. We work directly with family-run farms committed to chemical-free agriculture, ethical wages, and sustainable harvesting.

Direct Sourced

Direct relationships with organic estates across Coorg, Nilgiris & Darjeeling.

Artisan Craft

Roasted in micro-batches daily to preserve peak sweetness and aroma.

Whole Botanicals

Pure intact leaves, natural Meyer lemon, and crushed garden thyme herbs.
