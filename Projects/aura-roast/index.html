The Origin of AURA ROAST

Our Story — Handcrafted With Purpose

At AURA ROAST, we believe that life's most meaningful moments unfold over a warm cup or a chilled glass. What began as a local roastery driven by a passion for pure organic coffee has grown into an affordable celebration of everyday beverage rituals.

Today, we curate honest, beautifully crafted drinks at budget-friendly prices: single-estate coffees roasted daily to preserve natural caramel sugars, whole-leaf mountain teas infused with real herbs and citrus, and refreshing cold drinks crafted to revitalize your day without burning a hole in your pocket.

Direct Sourced

Direct relationships with organic estates across Coorg and Nilgiris.

Artisan Craft

Roasted in small micro-batches daily to preserve peak sweetness and aroma.

Pure Botanicals

Whole intact leaves, fresh lemon notes, and crushed garden thyme herbs.
