“What would humans have built instead?”

Removing IT does not regress humanity to the dark ages. Humans possess an unquenchable drive to coordinate, trade, diagnose, and preserve knowledge. In an alternate timeline where silicon microprocessors never materialized, civilization would have engineered elaborate physical, mechanical, and human-powered networks to bridge the distance.

SOCIETAL REPLACEMENTS

FIVE FOUNDATIONAL SYSTEMS

How would civilization execute essential human imperatives without software? Inspect the architectural divergence between our digital reality and the analog counterpart.

01 COMMUNICATION
HUMAN CONNECTION
OUR WORLD (WITH IT)
  • ✓ Real-time instant messaging apps (WhatsApp, Signal, Slack)
  • ✓ Global email routing and cloud inboxes
  • ✓ Low-latency HD video calling and virtual meetings
  • ✓ Global social media feeds and broadcast feeds
  • ✓ Frictionless, instantaneous planetary transmission
ALTERNATE WORLD (WITHOUT IT)
  • ✦ High-speed postal networks and pneumatic tube lines
  • ✦ Operator-switched copper telephone exchanges
  • ✦ Regional shortwave radio broadcasts and bulletin dispatches
  • ✦ Daily multi-edition printed newspapers and broadsheets
  • ✦ Physical civic communication halls and telegram stations
Core Insight: Humans would still need fast communication. The absence of IT would not remove that need — it would force society to construct elaborate physical and mechanical networks.
“Would communication become slower, or would humans invent another way to make distance feel smaller?”
02 BANKING & FINANCE
ECONOMIC TRUST
OUR WORLD (WITH IT)
  • ✓ Instant mobile payments (UPI, Apple Pay, digital wallets)
  • ✓ 24/7 online banking apps and cloud ledgers
  • ✓ Automated Teller Machines (ATMs) and contactless cards
  • ✓ Algorithmic high-frequency financial markets
  • ✓ Instantaneous global wire clearing (SWIFT, blockchain)
ALTERNATE WORLD (WITHOUT IT)
  • ✦ Bustling physical bank branches on every town high street
  • ✦ Hand-signed paper ledgers with ink stamps and embossed wax seals
  • ✦ National paper cheque clearinghouse networks
  • ✦ Verbal telephone verification with designated bank officers
  • ✦ Physical currency, specie vaults, and certified bearer bonds
Core Insight: Financial trust would rest entirely on institutional integrity, physical identity credentials, and personal banker relationships rather than cryptographic hashes.
“Would banking be slower — or would another physical system evolve to make transactions faster?”
03 HEALTHCARE & MEDICINE
HUMAN SURVIVAL
OUR WORLD (WITH IT)
  • ✓ Centralized Electronic Health Records (EHRs)
  • ✓ Video telemedicine and remote chronic patient monitoring
  • ✓ Cloud-assisted radiological imaging and AI cancer screening
  • ✓ Wearable biometric sensors and real-time heart trackers
  • ✓ Algorithmic genomic sequencing and digital pharmacology
ALTERNATE WORLD (WITHOUT IT)
  • ✦ Physical paper medical dossiers kept in localized clinic filing rooms
  • ✦ Direct in-person doctor-patient consultations and house calls
  • ✦ Manual chemical staining and laboratory ledgers
  • ✦ Physical appointment books and paper prescription pads
  • ✦ Stronger community dependence on regional hospital centers
Core Insight: Without remote telemetry, doctors would rely more on bedside diagnostic acumen, comprehensive family histories, and tactile examination.
“Would healthcare become more personal — but less connected?”
04 EDUCATION & KNOWLEDGE
COLLECTIVE MEMORY
OUR WORLD (WITH IT)
  • ✓ Online universities, MOOCs (Coursera, edX), and digital lectures
  • ✓ Virtual classrooms, interactive whiteboards, and LMS portals
  • ✓ Instant algorithmic search engines and automated summaries
  • ✓ Infinite digital libraries and digitized academic journals
  • ✓ Video tutorials, forums, and ubiquitous documentation
ALTERNATE WORLD (WITHOUT IT)
  • ✦ Physical lecture halls and seminar tables with blackboard demonstrations
  • ✦ Hand-bound printed textbooks, physical atlases, and encyclopedias
  • ✦ Master-curated municipal card catalogs and microfiche drawers
  • ✦ Structured postal correspondence courses and mailed assignments
  • ✦ Guild-based apprenticeship circles and community learning centers
Core Insight: Knowledge would be curated by specialized scholars and master librarians, eliminating digital hallucination at the expense of instant ubiquity.
“Would knowledge become harder to access, or would libraries become the world's most powerful infrastructure?”
05 COMMERCE & BUSINESS
VALUE EXCHANGE
OUR WORLD (WITH IT)
  • ✓ Global e-commerce marketplaces (Amazon, Shopify)
  • ✓ Cloud enterprise SaaS, ERPs, and automated accounting
  • ✓ Algorithmic targeted digital ads and social marketing
  • ✓ Distributed asynchronous remote work teams
  • ✓ Fully automated just-in-time global supply chains
ALTERNATE WORLD (WITHOUT IT)
  • ✦ Vibrant local retail storefronts, cooperatives, and high streets
  • ✦ Double-entry paper accounting ledgers and adding machines
  • ✦ Telephone sales desks, trade print catalogs, and mail orders
  • ✦ Physical office headquarters and shared executive chambers
  • ✦ Resilient regional supplier networks and local distributor rings
Core Insight: Without algorithmic global dominance, local merchant ecosystems flourish. Economic wealth circulates within regional boundaries.
“Would business become slower — or would local economies become stronger?”
CIVILIZATION CALCULUS

REMOVING IT DOESN'T REMOVE PROBLEMS.
IT REPLACES THEM.

Every infrastructure design choice represents a philosophical trade-off. Inspect the bilateral ledger between the digital universe and the analog alternative.

WITH IT (DIGITAL ACCELERATION)
WITHOUT IT (PHYSICAL RESILIENCE)
⚡ Faster communication: Instant messages span continents in milliseconds.
✦ More physical interaction: Real face-to-face presence, handshake agreements, voice nuances.
⚡ Global access: Planetary reach for ideas, media, and distributed teams.
✦ Stronger local dependence: Tight-knit municipal networks, neighborhood self-reliance.
⚡ Autonomous automation: Machines handle computational grunt work.
✦ More human labour: High demand for skilled clerks, craftsmen, and physical operators.
⚡ Digital convenience: One-tap grocery orders, instant balance transfers.
✦ Manual processes: Deliberate paperwork, structured schedules, tangible receipts.
⚡ Global monopolies: Megacorporations dominate continental market shares.
✦ Local businesses: Independent retailers, regional distributors, community banks.
⚡ Remote work freedom: Geographically distributed lifestyle and flexibility.
✦ Location-dependent work: Shared civic workplaces, clear boundary between job and home.
⚡ Massive information access: Petabytes of indexed knowledge at every fingertips.
✦ Curated information: Editorial authority, peer-verified volumes, absence of algorithmic outrage.
⚡ Cybercrime & Ransomware: Distributed denial-of-service, identity theft, algorithmic surveillance.
✦ Physical fraud & theft: Forged signatures, vault burglaries, paper counterfeit risks.

“Removing IT would not create a perfect world.
It would create a different world.”
