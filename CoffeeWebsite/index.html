The Aurelia Story

Coffee should never feel anonymous.

Most coffee reaches the customer as a finished package with very little story behind it.

Aurelia takes a different approach. We connect the journey of the bean with the experience of the customer — from sourcing and roasting to quality verification and final delivery.

“Know your coffee. Taste your difference.” Our philosophy behind every Aurelia product.

From Bean To Cup

The journey behind every Aurelia pack.

We divide production into clearly monitored stages. This helps us protect flavour, freshness and consistency.

01

Source

Coffee is selected from responsible growing regions based on quality and flavour potential.

02

Inspect

Beans are checked for physical quality, consistency and batch characteristics.

03

Roast

Small batches are roasted according to the intended flavour profile.

04

Verify

Quality information is recorded before the product moves to packaging.

05

Deliver

Freshly sealed coffee reaches customers through our distribution network.

Our People

Great coffee is a team product.

Aurelia's workflow is divided across specialist teams. Every department has a role in turning an idea into the final customer experience.

01

Sourcing Team

Finds coffee origins, works with suppliers and evaluates incoming beans.

02

Roasting Team

Develops roast profiles and ensures each coffee expresses its intended character.

03

Quality Team

Checks batches and verifies that production standards are maintained.

04

Technology Team

Builds the digital systems that support product information and customer interaction.

05

Packaging Team

Protects the product through carefully designed packaging and freshness control.

06

Marketing Team

Communicates the brand story and connects the product with the right audience.

07

Customer Team

Collects feedback and helps customers choose the right coffee for their preferences.

08

Operations Team

Coordinates inventory, orders, logistics and the overall business workflow.
